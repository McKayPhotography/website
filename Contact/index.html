Investment Guide

Every session below includes fully hand-retouched high-resolution digital files with a print release for personal use. Mini, Basic, and Full sessions are built for individuals, couples, or small groups and cap at 4 people total (1 + up to 3 additional). Families of 5 or more should book Small Family & Legacy or Multi-Generational instead of stacking add-on fees.

Mini Session

$75

30 min · 2–3 poses · 5–10 edited images · 1 person (+$20 per additional, up to 3). A headshot refresh, quick single-subject update, or lead-in offer for new clients.

Basic Session

$150

60 min · 3–6 poses · 15–20 edited images · 1 person (+$25 per additional, up to 3). A standard senior portrait, individual, or couple session.

Full Session

$300

90–120 min · multiple setups · 40 edited images · 1 person (+$40 per additional, up to 3). Senior portraits, engagement, or in-depth individual/couple sessions.

Small Family & Legacy

$300

90 min · multiple setups · 40–45 edited images · up to 6 people, flat rate. Parents + kids, styled around a single unhurried session.

Multi-Generational

$500

2–3 hrs · multiple setups/locations · 60+ edited images · up to 8 people (+$25 per additional beyond 8). Reunions, extended family, and milestone gatherings.
